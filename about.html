About Us

FEU Diliman Library is a space dedicated to supporting the academic and personal growth of our students. We offer a wide range of books, resources, and digital tools to enhance learning and creativity. Our mission is to foster a love for reading, encourage independent learning, and provide a quiet space for exploration and discovery.

Our Vision

To be a hub of knowledge and discovery, fostering a love of reading, learning, and community engagement for all generations.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to provide accessible, inclusive, and diverse resources that empower individuals, inspire lifelong learning, and contribute to the intellectual and cultural growth of our community.

Our Values

  • Access & Inclusivity
  • Education & Learning
  • Innovation
  • Community
  • Integrity

Our Goals

  1. Provide a welcoming and inclusive environment for all patrons.
  2. Offer a diverse collection of resources that reflect the needs and interests of our community.
  3. Support lifelong learning and literacy through programs and services.
  4. Engage with our community through partnerships and outreach.
  5. Ensure the sustainability and growth of our library for future generations.

1983

FEU Diliman Campus Opens

FEU Diliman is established in Quezon City on a 10-hectare site for the Institute of Technology, serving engineering students. The library begins as a basic resource center for these programs.

1994

FEU-FERN College Rebranding

FEU Diliman rebrands as FEU-FERN College to mark Dr. Nicanor Reyes Sr.’s centennial, expanding to Accountancy, Business, IT, and Basic Education. The library grows to support this wider academic scope.

1990s

Tech Integration at Diliman

With IT programs strengthening at FEU Diliman, the library likely starts incorporating digital tools, reflecting the campus’s focus on technology-driven education.

2000s

Electronic Library Takes Shape

The FEU Diliman Library introduces an Electronic Library, offering online resources to meet the needs of students from Basic Education to College at this campus.

2010s

Serving Kinder to College

As FEU Diliman’s Basic Education Department grows, including the CLICK program for coding and creativity, the library adapts with resources for both young learners and college students.

2025

Diliman's Modern Library Hub

Located in FEU Diliman’s Administration Building, the library now features Circulation, Filipiniana, and an advanced Electronic Library, supporting the campus’s diverse community.
